# Architecture & Technical Decisions Log

- **D001 — Lean asyncio orchestrator over full Pipecat pipeline:** Chose a lean custom `asyncio` + FastAPI WebSocket orchestrator wrapping direct Gnani Prisma v3.0 / Timbre v3.0 WebSocket adapters rather than full Pipecat pipeline orchestration. Reason: live verification showed `pipecat-gnani==0.6.1` does not enable `x-partials: true` or parse Prisma v3.0's `partial_transcript`/`final_transcript` event types, and a direct `asyncio` loop gives deterministic hard-interrupt danger-sign escalation and clean JSON/PCM envelopes for Android (Agent 2) without fighting Pipecat's protobuf serializer. Alternative: custom `FrameProcessor` + `AndroidJsonFrameSerializer` inside Pipecat.
- **D002 — Canonical 16 kHz mono 16-bit PCM (`pcm_s16le`) at 32 ms frames:** Standardised client <-> backend <-> Prisma <-> Timbre on 16 kHz, 16-bit LE mono PCM (512 samples = 1024 bytes). Reason: natively supported by Silero VAD, Prisma v3.0, and Timbre v3.0 with zero transcoding overhead. Alternative: 8 kHz telephony or 48 kHz WebRTC audio with server-side resampling.
- **D003 — Zero-torch ONNX Silero VAD with dual-gate RMS barge-in:** Load `silero_vad.onnx` via `onnxruntime` directly and require both speech probability and SNR-above-noise-floor energy sustained over `BARGE_IN_MIN_SPEECH_MS` to trigger barge-in. Reason: avoids PyTorch dependency bloat while rejecting transient household/street noise bursts. Alternative: server-side VAD only or `silero-vad` with PyTorch.
- **D004 — `indic-auto` + `codemix` + ASHA domain `x-keywords` on Prisma v3.0:** Configure Prisma v3.0 streaming STT with `lang_code=indic-auto`, `x-language-candidates` from `SUPPORTED_LANGUAGES`, `x-format=codemix`, `x-partials=true`, and ASHA clinical keywords. Reason: live probe confirmed 146–157 ms finalization latency and native English medical term preservation alongside Indic scripts. Alternative: fixed single-language STT requiring manual language switches.
- **D005 — Socket-reset cancellation + monotonic `turn_id` on Timbre v3.0:** Cancel active TTS on barge-in by incrementing `active_turn_id`, closing the Timbre WS, dropping stale chunks, and emitting `tts_cancelled`. Reason: matches `pipecat-gnani`'s `InterruptibleTTSService` behaviour and guarantees zero stale audio playback. Alternative: waiting for Timbre `complete` and discarding locally (wastes bandwidth and latency).
