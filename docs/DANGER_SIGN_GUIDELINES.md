# Official Indian Public-Health Guidelines for Maternal and Newborn Danger Signs

This reference document compiles **verified danger signs, clinical thresholds, and referral rules** for **Antenatal (ANC)**, **Intranatal/Delivery**, **Postnatal/Postpartum (PNC)**, and **Newborn (HBNC)** care as taught to and used by **ASHAs (Accredited Social Health Activists)** under India's **National Health Mission (NHM)** and **Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW)**.

All entries below were verified directly against primary-source official NHM/MoHFW/NHSRC PDFs retrieved during research. No clinical thresholds were inferred or extrapolated beyond what is explicitly stated in the cited documents.

| **Severe headache and/or blurred vision (Pre-eclampsia)** | Mother | ANC | Severe headache with blurred vision, spots before the eyes, or dizziness. | **Severe / continuous abdominal pain** | Mother | ANC | Severe or continuous pain in the abdomen; in `MOD6` paired with loss of fetal movement; in `INT-ASHA-2026` also flagged for previous CS scar tenderness. | None beyond severity/persistence. | `MOD6` Part B §4, p. 30 (PDF p. 31); `INT-ASHA-2026` §B.4.3, pp. 177, 182–183 (PDF pp. 187, 192–193); `DAKSHATA` SCC CHECK-1, p. 14 (PDF p. 28). |
| **High fever in pregnancy** | Mother | ANC | High fever during pregnancy (`MCP-CARD`, `INT-ASHA-2026`, `DAKSHATA`). | `MOD6` p. 32: `Temp > 100°F (37.8°C)` (see Discrepancies: `MOD6` treats as non-emergency with paracetamol/48h follow-up, whereas `MCP-CARD` & `INT-ASHA-2026` treat high fever as immediate danger sign); `DAKSHATA` SCC CHECK-1: `≥ 38°C (≥ 100.5°F)` for antibiotics + referral. | `MOD6` Part B §4, p. 32 (PDF p. 33); `MCP-CARD` PDF p. 4; `MCP-GB` §6, p. 15 (PDF p. 19); `INT-ASHA-2026` §B.4.3, pp. 177, 183 (PDF pp. 187, 193); `DAKSHATA` SCC CHECK-1, p. 14 (PDF p. 28). |

| **Premature rupture of membranes (water bag bursting without labour pains)** | Mother | ANC | Bursting/leaking of amniotic fluid ("water bag") before due date or without labour pains. | `DAKSHATA` SCC CHECK-1: Rupture of membranes `< 37 weeks` gestation OR `> 12 hrs` without labour (antibiotic indication). | `MCP-CARD` PDF p. 4; `MCP-GB` §6, p. 15 (PDF p. 19); `INT-ASHA-2026` §B.4.3, p. 183 (PDF p. 193); `DAKSHATA` SCC CHECK-1, p. 14 (PDF p. 28). |
| **Pre-term labour pains** | Mother | ANC | Labour pains starting before the due date / before term. | `< 37 weeks` gestation (`MCP-GB` p. 15); `MOD7` defines pre-term as `≤ 8 months and 14 days` (p. 49). | `MCP-GB` §6, p. 15 (PDF p. 19); `INT-ASHA-2026` §B.4.3, p. 183 (PDF p. 193); `MOD7` Part C §1, p. 49 (PDF p. 51). |

| **Pain/burning while urinating** | Mother | ANC | Dysuria / urinary frequency / urgency. | `MOD6` p. 32 advises fluids and referral to PHC if no relief after `24 hours`; `INT-ASHA-2026` pp. 177, 183 lists it among danger signs requiring referral. | `MOD6` Part B §4, p. 32 (PDF p. 33); `INT-ASHA-2026` §B.4.3, pp. 177, 183 (PDF pp. 187, 193). |
| **Excessive/persistent vomiting or jaundice in pregnancy** | Mother | ANC | Excessive vomiting or vomiting continuing into 2nd trimester; yellowish eyes (jaundice). | Refer to AAM / higher facility if vomiting is excessive or continues into 2nd trimester (`INT-ASHA-2026` p. 175), or if jaundice is observed (`p. 177`). | `INT-ASHA-2026` §B.4.2–B.4.3, pp. 175, 177, 182 (PDF pp. 185, 187, 192). |

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| **Fits / convulsions / unconsciousness, fever, or swollen face & hands during labour** | Mother | Intranatal | Convulsions, loss of consciousness, fever, or facial/hand oedema during labour. | `DAKSHATA` SCC CHECK-2: maternal temp `≥ 38°C (≥ 100.5°F)` for antibiotics; MgSO₄/FRU referral for severe BP/symptoms/convulsions. | `MOD6` Part B §4, p. 33 (PDF p. 34) & Annexe 4, p. 66 (PDF p. 67); `DAKSHATA` SCC CHECK-2, p. 15 (PDF p. 29). |

## 3. Scope Limits, Discrepancies Between Sources & Implementation Implications

1. **Neonatal Sepsis Diagnostic Rule (`MOD6`/`MOD7` vs. `HBNC-2014`):**
   - `MOD7` (Annexe 1, p. 72) lists **7 signs of sepsis** and requires **at least 2 criteria present on the same day** to diagnose sepsis (RR > 60/min is not in the 7-item box).
   - `HBNC-2014` (Annexure 1c §E, p. 22 & Modifications, p. 28) explicitly **adds an 8th criterion** (*"Respiratory rate more than 60 per minute"*) and lowers the action threshold to **"If any one feature of sepsis are present on the same day, diagnose as sepsis and start with first dose of Amoxicillin... and refer the newborn immediately to SNCU"**.
   - **Safety Rule for Voice Engine:** Follow the stricter `HBNC-2014` single-sign escalation rule (any **one** verified sepsis/danger sign triggers urgent referral).

2. **Newborn Temperature Thresholds:**
   - **Fever:** `MOD6`/`MOD7`/`HBNC-2014` define newborn fever as **`> 99°F (37.2°C)`**; `INT-ASHA-2026` uses **`≥ 99°F (37.2°C)`**; `DAKSHATA` facility SCC uses **`> 38°C`**; `HBYC` (2 months–5 years) uses **`≥ 37.5°C (99.5°F)`**.
   - **Hypothermia:** `MOD6` & `INT-ASHA-2026` define mild cold stress as **`< 97°F (36.1°C)`** and severe hypothermia as **`< 95°F (35.0°C)`**; `HBNC-2014` p. 21 & `MOD7` p. 71 cite **`< 95.9°F (35.5°C)`** as the lower action cutoff; `DAKSHATA` SCC uses **`< 36°C` and not rising after warming**.
   - **Safety Rule:** Flag any reported `cold to touch`, `< 36.1°C / 97°F` (advise warming + reassess), and treat `< 35.5°C / 95.9°F` (or cold to touch with lethargy/poor feeding) as immediate danger-sign escalation.

3. **Prolonged Labour Duration Threshold (`12 hrs` vs. `8 hrs` vs. `24 hrs`):**
   - `MCP-CARD` (PDF p. 4) & `MCP-GB` (p. 15): labour pain **`> 12 hours`**.
   - `MOD6` (p. 33): pushing **`> 12 hours`** (primipara) or **`> 8 hours`** (multipara).
   - `MOD6` Annexe 4 (p. 66) & `DAKSHATA` SCC CHECK-1 (p. 14): **`> 24 hours`** from onset of mild labour.
   - **Safety Rule:** Escalate conservatively at `> 12 hours` of labour pain (or `> 8 hours` if multipara).

4. **Postpartum Bleeding Thresholds:**
   - `MOD6` / `MOD7` / `HBNC-2014` / `INT-ASHA-2026` (community home visits): **`> 5 pads/day`** or **`> 1 thick cloth/day`**.
   - `INT-ASHA-2026` (p. 48): **`> 2–3 pads in 20–30 minutes`**.
   - `DAKSHATA` SCC CHECK-3 (facility): **`≥ 500 ml`** or **`1 pad soaked in < 5 minutes`**.
   - Any of these—or qualitative reports of heavy/uncontrolled bleeding—must trigger immediate urgent escalation.

5. **Fever During Pregnancy (`MOD6` vs. `MCP-CARD` / `INT-ASHA-2026`):**
   - `MOD6` (p. 32) places `Temp > 100°F (37.8°C)` in the *Non-Emergency referral* table (paracetamol, refer if no relief in 48h), whereas `MCP-CARD` (PDF p. 4) and `INT-ASHA-2026` (pp. 177, 183) classify **high fever during pregnancy** as an **immediate danger sign** requiring urgent hospital evaluation (`DAKSHATA` SCC CHECK-1 uses `≥ 38°C / 100.5°F`).
   - **Safety Rule:** Treat reported high fever in pregnancy conservatively as an immediate danger sign.

6. **High-Risk Factors vs. Acute Danger Signs (`INT-ASHA-2026` §B.1.11 & §B.4.3):**
   - `INT-ASHA-2026` explicitly distinguishes **biological "At-Risk" criteria** (e.g., newborn `< 2500 g` / preterm `< 37 wks` without symptoms; or High-Risk Pregnancy factors like teenage/elderly primi, previous LSCS, Rh-negative) — which require **extra follow-up visits and FRU birth-plan linkage** — from **acute Danger Signs** requiring **immediate emergency referral**. Notably, birth weight **`< 1.8 kg`** (`MOD6`, `HBNC-2014`) and **`Hb < 7.0 g/dl`** (`MOD6`, `INT-ASHA-2026`) DO mandate immediate referral.

7. **Scope Boundary for Voice Assistant:**
   - ASHA guidelines mandate **early identification, basic stabilization/pre-referral counsel (e.g., skin-to-skin warmth/KMC during transport, initiating breastfeeding for postpartum bleeding where appropriate), and immediate referral to PHC/FRU/CHC/SNCU/DH**.
   - The voice assistant must **never diagnose, prescribe antibiotics/medications, recommend home remedies for danger signs, or rule out danger**.
